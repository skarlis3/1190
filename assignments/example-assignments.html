Example Assignments

This page links to a shared folder of example student work from past sections of ENGL 1190. The folder is hosted on Macomb's OneDrive, so you'll need to be signed in with your Macomb student account to view the files.

Before You Click

Make sure you're signed into your Macomb student account in your browser. If you try to open the folder while signed into a personal Microsoft account, OneDrive will tell you that you don't have access. Sign out of any personal accounts first, or open the link in a private/incognito window and sign in with your Macomb credentials.

Open the Example Folder

Open the Example Assignments folder on OneDrive (opens in a new tab; sign in with your Macomb account)

How to Use the Examples

The folder includes examples of student work across different assignments. As you look through them, keep these things in mind:

  • Examples are meant to give you a sense of what the assignment can look like — not a template to copy. Your project should be your own.
  • Different students take different approaches. If something in an example doesn't match what you're planning, that doesn't mean either approach is wrong.
  • Pay attention to structure and organization as much as content — how the writer set things up is often more useful than the specific topic they chose.
  • If you have questions about why an example does something a certain way, bring them to class or office hours.

Trouble Accessing the Folder?

If the link doesn't work, try these steps in order:

  1. Make sure you're signed into your Macomb student account at office.com (opens in a new tab) before clicking the folder link.
  2. If you're already signed in with a personal Microsoft account, sign out and try again with your Macomb account.
  3. Try opening the folder in a private/incognito browser window and signing in with your Macomb credentials there.
  4. If none of that works, message me through Canvas and I'll help troubleshoot.
